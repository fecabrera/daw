MP3 export uses LAME 3.100 through mp3lame-encoder 0.2.5 and mp3lame-sys 0.1.11.
The encoder is compiled into the application. License texts are included here.

Source and build information:
https://github.com/DoumanAsh/mp3lame-encoder
https://github.com/DoumanAsh/mp3lame-sys
https://lame.sourceforge.io/

Exact dependency source archives are available from crates.io:
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/mp3lame-encoder/0.2.5/download
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/mp3lame-sys/0.1.11/download
The mp3lame-sys archive includes the LAME source.
Cargo.lock records the exact versions. Desktop packages include DAW-source.zip,
SOURCE-ACCESS.txt and source-manifest.json beside the executable (under
DAW.app/Contents/Resources on macOS). The source archive contains the exact
application and vendored Cargo dependencies, including these LAME sources.
Read SOURCE-BUILD.txt in the extracted DAW directory to rebuild/relink.
