Audio decoding uses Symphonia 0.5.5 crates, including the MP3 and
AAC-LC decoders, RIFF/WAV, Ogg and ISO MP4 readers, metadata and Xiph utilities.

Source: https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia/tree/v0.5.5
Exact source archives: https://crates.io/crates/symphonia/0.5.5/dependencies
Registry dependency versions and archive checksums are recorded in Cargo.lock.
The original source is available from crates.io, for example:
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/symphonia-codec-aac/0.5.5/download
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/symphonia-format-isomp4/0.5.5/download
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/symphonia-utils-xiph/0.5.5/download

Ogg Opus decoding uses the separately licensed libopus adapter; see Opus-NOTICE.txt.

The ISO MP4 reader has a local resource-bound patch. It validates atom/table
extents and limits metadata and packet storage before fallible allocation.
Original decoder algorithms remain unchanged. The exact modified MPL-2.0 source
and patch provenance are included in the corresponding-source archive under
vendor/symphonia-format-isomp4, including DAW-SOURCE-NOTICE.md and LICENSE.
